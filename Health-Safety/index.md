<section class="page-shell section">
  <h2 class="section-title">Health &amp; Safety</h2>
  <p>This page is the shared Health &amp; Safety home for all course pages.</p>

  <div class="content-body">
    <h3>Before Practical Work</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Read workshop or classroom safety instructions for your subject area.</li>
      <li>Wear the required PPE and secure loose clothing or hair.</li>
      <li>Check tools and equipment before use, and report faults immediately.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>During Activities</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Follow teacher instructions and safety signage at all times.</li>
      <li>Use tools only for their intended purpose.</li>
      <li>Keep your workspace tidy and walkways clear.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Emergency and Reporting</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Tell a teacher immediately about any injury, spill, or unsafe situation.</li>
      <li>Know the location of exits, first-aid kits, and emergency shutoff points.</li>
      <li>Record incidents using the school process when instructed.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
